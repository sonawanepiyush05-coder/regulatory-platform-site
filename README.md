## 📌 Overview

Navigating medical device regulations (such as US FDA, EU MDR, CDSCO, and international ISO standards) often involves fragmented documentation, costly paywalls, and complex compliance frameworks. 

**ReguDocs** consolidates essential regulatory knowledge into an intuitive platform featuring:
* Progressive, topic-by-topic learning roadmaps.
* Downloadable, production-ready compliance templates and SOPs.
* Centralized guides for global quality management and safety standards.

---

## ✨ Features

### 🎓 1. Structured Learning Roadmaps
Sequential modules categorized from beginner to advanced:
* **Medical Device Basics & Classification:** Understanding Risk Classes (Class I, IIa, IIb, III).
* **Quality Management Systems:** Deep dive into ISO 13485 requirements.
* **Risk Management:** ISO 14971 hazard analysis, risk control, and evaluation.
* **Clinical & Post-Market Operations:** Clinical Evaluation Reports (CER), Post-Market Surveillance (PMS), and PMCF frameworks.

### 📁 2. Standardized Templates & SOPs
Curated repository of ready-to-use regulatory formats with preview and direct download capabilities:
* **Submissions:** Device Master File (DMF) templates tailored for CDSCO submissions.
* **Risk & Safety:** ISO 14971-compliant Risk Management Files (RMF) and hazard logs.
* **Clinical Documentation:** Clinical Evaluation Reports (CER) and Post-Market Clinical Follow-up (PMCF) plans.
* **Technical & Manufacturing:** Instructions For Use (IFU), IQ/OQ/PQ Validation Protocols, and Standard Operating Procedures (SOPs).

### 🔍 3. Real-Time Resource Discovery
* Client-side search bar for instant lookups across standards, templates, and regulatory modules.
* Category filtering (`All`, `Templates`, `SOPs`, `Validation`, `Forms`).
